Race

Early Offense
"No Layups, 3s or Fouls in first 6 seconds"

Beliefs / Playbook

  • ★
    Pace 21 ➔ Cross 1/2 by 21
  • ★
    Spots ➔ 5 Out or 4 Out
  • ★
    Early Paint ➔ Drive or Rim Run
  • ★
    Blender ➔ Force Rotation
  • ★
    Drag (Fist) = Transition PNR
  • ★
    77 = Double Drag

Early Offense Terms

Pace 21
Get the ball transported across half court by 21 seconds on the shot clock—either through breakout dribble or throw ahead.
Spots
All players sprint to the 5 spots (Deep Corners, Wings, & Post) in 5 seconds or less. Post is either Big Ahead into Dunker or Big Above as a Trailer.
Dunker
Short corner. "Heels on the baseline", outside the hash mark, wide and flat. Spaced opposite the ball handler.
Throw Ahead
Look to advance the ball with the pass. Create early numbers advantages before the defense can set up.
Deep Outlet
Guard gets butt to the sideline at Free Throw Line Extended. First sneak a peek down court to see numbers before receiving pass.
Closest Man Inbounds
If the 5 man inbounds, he trails and we are 5 Out (Big Above). If anyone else inbounds, the 5 rim runs (Big Ahead) and we set 4 Out spacing.
Drag
Trailing post player sets a transition pick & roll for the ball handler to use immediately after a defensive miss.
77
Double Drag. A staggered ball screen in transition where O5 sets the second screen.

Secondary Breaks
"Triggers if we are neutralized"

Point Guard Calls

  • ★
    Horns Side = 5 Out Pistol
  • ★
    "4" = 4 Out DHO into Step Up
  • ★
    "1" = 5 Out Single Pin
  • ★
    "2" = Stagger
  • ★
    "0" = 5 Out Split (O5 Handling)
  • ★
    "4 Down" = Block Mover
  • ★
    "Motion" / Finger Twirl = Continuous PNR

Offense Core Terms

Neutralized
If defense has all 5 back, loaded to the ball, and on-ball defender is square with no foot angle advantage. Flow into a Trigger immediately.
Trigger
Screening action (one of Core 5 calls) incorporating O5 to get a ballhandler downhill, forcing defense into rotation.
Big Ahead
When O5 rim runs ahead of the ball. If he doesn't receive it early, he spaces to opposite dunker and we run the "4" series (4 Out spacing).
Big Above
When O5 is above the 3-point line (trailing). We initiate any of our 5-Out Triggers called early by the ball handler.
Early Call
Point Guard identifies if we are in 4 Out or 5 Out spacing, assesses if neutralized, and makes a call for a Core Trigger.

Drive & Kick

Paint Attacks
.5 Scramble Multiple Attacks

Attack Phases

  • ★
    1st Side = Trigger
  • ★
    2nd Side = 2-Man Game
  • ★
    3rd Side = Flash or Snap

Drive & Kick Terms

Break the Paint
Any time we enter the paint with a drive or pass. Must seek to engage two defenders to open up team options.
Scramble
Forcing the defense into full rotation by pulling their LOW MAN up to help at the rim, causing double teams.
E2 (Engage 2)
Stands for Engage 2. When this happens, you must look to pass quickly to the open teammate.
Drive Kick Swing
Drive the paint, kick out, and make a quick swing pass to punish defensive rotation (making the "Extra Pass").
Kick Out Get Out
If you drive into the paint and kick the ball out, you must sprint and exit cut faster than you entered. Restores spacing for next attack.
.5 (Half-Second)
Once defense is scrambling, decide to shoot, drive, or pass in 0.5 seconds. Do not let the ball stick or hold.

PNR Techniques & Attack Reads

Screening Basics
Sprint to Screen: "Arrive Alone" to get separation.
Screen Lower 1/3: Screen lower body to "Force the Over".
Touch & Go: Slip screen immediately as defender hips turn.
Drop/Over Attack
Hostage: Seal defender on back.
Hang: Float dribble to freeze drop big.
Gortat: Hostage O1 on back, seal X5 at rim.
Engage Big: Pull X5 to open roll pocket.
Drop/Under Attack
Twist: Re-screen if defense goes under.
Race the Under: Beat under defender to paint.
Ice Attack
Slip Roll: Dive screener to rim on Ice.
Twist Screen: Flip into step up screen.
Get Game: Pop big, handback going over top.
Switch Attack
Race Switch: Drive soft switch downhill.
Cutback (Drive Wake): Attack switch foot angle.
Boomerang: Pass on switch, back up, catch and drive wake.
Matchup Calls
Mouse in House: Small guarding big.
Moose on Loose: Big guarding guard on perimeter.

Post Feed & Cutters

Inside-Out Game
"Catch-Chin-Check"

Post Actions

  • ★
    Catch-Chin-Check ➔ 2 Feet, 2 Hands, check shoulder
  • ★
    Score-Search-Skip ➔ Score, find cutter, skip opposite
  • ★
    Blast ➔ Uphill DHO to slip roll/pop

Post Game Terms

Speed Cut
Post feeder cuts baseline side of post player, sprints to basket, and out to opposite corner.
Catch-Chin-Check
Catch on two feet with two hands, chin the ball to protect it, and check over inside shoulder for cutters.
Score-Search-Skip
Look to score first, search for cutting guards second, skip opposite for 3 if defense collapses.
Blast
If post has no advantage, DHO uphill to perimeter teammate and slip roll/pop.

Transition Terms

2 BACK
2 guards get back (through elbows if in corner) during shot attempts to stop leak outs and head men.
2 BASKET
2 Bigs go rebound shot. If no Oreb then must sprint back while locating matchup.
SCRAM

Shell Team Terms

BALL
Must point and declare ball on every catch.
STRONG
Defender guarding player next to ball. Responsible for shrinking and stunting.
RIM
Defender responsible for RIM HELP on attacks. Learn how to stunt for help.
NAIL
Top weak side defender. Sinks on baseline or slot drive, has 1st pass out.
B.E.N
Our shrink spots: Blocks, Elbows, and Nail. Shrink floor when not guarding ball.

Pick and Roll Defense

PNR Coverages
"THE 5 Ts: TALK, TOUCH, TRACE, TAKE, TAG"

The 5 Ts Framework

  • ★
    TALK: Big calls early (3x). "DROP", "ICE", "RED".
  • ★
    TOUCH: Guard impacts ball; Big up to touch.
  • ★
    TRACE: Big takes low pocket pass; Guard high.

PNR Coverages & Tactics

DROP (Zone Drop)
Big zones and drops toward basket. Plays centerfield between ball and rim.
ICE
On outer 1/3 of floor. Guard prevents ball from going middle. Big drops.
HOT (Blitz)
Hard double team on ball until dribble is picked up or retreat dribble.
RED (Switch)
Contact switch on screen. Screener's man calls RED early.

GOLD (Base Post)
3/4 Top Denial. Slide behind on the pass. Wall off the shot and show hands.
NO MIDDLE

DIG HELP

Special Situations

End of Game & Out of Bounds
"ASSIGNMENT EXCELLENCE"

Beliefs / Playbook

  • ★
    ASSIGNMENT EXCELLENCE

Situational Rules

EOG UP 2
"STAY BEFORE - SWITCH AFTER". No switches before inbound. Switch everything after ball is inbounded.
EOG UP 3
"SWITCH BEFORE AND AFTER". Switch all screens before inbound and after. Chop when possible to prevent 3s.
CHOP (Intentional Foul)
Foul when up by 3 points, or at end of quarter when we have a foul to give.
