Early Offence Terms

1-3-8
Inbound the ball in 1 sec, ball crosses half (pass ahead or dribble) in 3 secs, shoot a C&S 3, Key, Free throw in 7 seconds.
Spots
All players sprint (WIDE then LONG) to the 5 spots (Deep Corners, Wings, & Post) in 5 seconds or less. Post is either Big Ahead into Dunker or Big Above as a Trailer.
Dunker
Short corner. "Heels on the baseline", outside the hash mark, wide and flat. Spaced weakside.
Head Man
Look to advance the ball with the pass. Create early numbers advantages before the defense can set up.
Deep Outlet
Guard gets butt to the sideline at Free Throw Line Extended. First sneak a peek down court to see numbers before receiving pass.
Closest Man Inbounds/Outlets
If the 5 man inbounds, he trails and we are 5 Out (Big Above). If anyone else inbounds, the 5 rim runs (Big Ahead) and we set 4 Out spacing.
Fist
Trailing post player sets a transition pick & roll for the ball handler to use immediately after a defensive miss.
2up
Double Drag. A staggered ball screen in transition where O5 sets the second screen.
C/Step/None/Fist
Links ⇒ Chains, Letters ⇒ Words
C Cut Outlet
Guard can't get the deep outlet, he cuts towards the ball in a curved way that keeps his momentum going towards the halfcourt.

Offense Core Terms

Neutralized
If defense has all 5 back, loaded to the ball, and on-ball defender is square with no foot angle advantage. Flow into a Trigger immediately.
Trigger
Screening action incorporating O5 to get a ballhandler downhill, forcing defense into rotation.
Big Ahead
When O5 rim runs ahead of the ball. If he doesn't receive it early, he spaces to opposite dunker and we run the "4" series (4 Out spacing).
Big Above
When O5 is above the 3-point line (trailing). We initiate any of our 5-Out Triggers called early by the ball handler.
Early Call
Point Guard identifies if we are in 4 Out or 5 Out spacing, assesses if neutralized, and makes a call for a Core Trigger.

Drive & Kick Terms

Grid Spacing
A half-court framework dividing the offensive zone into six sectors by extending the free-throw elbows to the sideline and halfcourt. Two players may only occupy the same sector during active, purposeful play-actions (screens, hand-offs, cuts). Violations kill spacing.
Paint Touches
Any time we enter the paint with a drive or pass. Must seek to engage two defenders to open up team options.
Scramble
Forcing the defense into full rotation by pulling their Drim up to help causing double teams (E2).
E2 (Engage 2)
Stands for Engage 2. When this happens, you must look to pass quickly to the open teammate.
Drive Kick Swing
Drive the paint, kick out, and make a quick swing pass to punish defensive rotation (making the "Extra Pass").
Kick Out Get Out (KOGO)
If you drive into the paint and kick the ball out, you must sprint and exit cut faster than you entered. Restores spacing for next attack.
.5 (Half-Second)
Once defense is scrambling, decide to shoot, drive, or pass in 0.5 seconds. Do not let the ball stick or hold.

Flow & Action Tag Terms

1st Side / 2nd Side
1st: The initial trigger side. O5 rolls and spaces to opposite dunker.
2nd: Ball crosses midline after 1st side. We run a 2-man game.
Snap / Shark / Bounce
Snap (Ghost): Fake screen and slip to 3-point line.
Shark: Pitch handoff.
Bounce: Dribble handoff (DHO).
Hand / Grenade or Rifle?
Hand: Handback PNR with O5.
Grenade/Rifle: Weakside corner big flashes out for Get game with small in slot.
Step / Elbow Hand
Step: Slot Step-up Screen.
Elbow Hand: Big flashes from dunker to elbow for high or low splits.
Up / Side
Up: Have the trigger at the top of the 3.
Side: Shift trigger to wing.
Twist / Flip (Coin Flip)
Twist: Re-screening to punish defence going under.
Flip: Screener switches screen side last minute.
Stack / Cash
Stack: Spain PNR backscreen.
Cash: Backdoor cut against overplay. Face cut if flat, backcut if up-line.
Burn / Jazz / Dive
Burn: Baseline cut when ball screen is happening.
Jazz: Wing cut when ball screen is happening.
Dive: When you see the back of your defender's head or are top locked, immediately cut hard to the basket.
Exit (Klay Action)
Miami baseline pin screen while PNR occurs, pulling tags away from the roller.
Choke / Hammer
Choke: Back screen.
Hammer: Weakside flare screen during baseline drive.
Split Action
Me: Receiver back doors to rim.
You: Screener back doors to rim.
Sling: Receiver curls around screener.
Straight: Receiver comes off the down screen.
0 / Step / Fist / C
0: Ball screen where handler is going toward open side corner.
Step: Ball screen where handler is going toward 1 teammate.
Fist: Ball screen where handler is going toward 2 teammates.
C: Ball screen where handler is going toward 3 teammates.

Closeout Reads

Attack Advantages
.5 Decisions

Beliefs / Playbook

    Offensive Closeout Terms

    4 Point Line Spacing
    Space 3 feet behind the FIBA line in slots. Forces nail defender to cover longer distance, opening drive lines.
    Stampede (Sweep and "Go")
    Catch on the run and rip through opposite of closeout to puncture gaps immediately.
    Run Thru / Cash (Catch n Go)
    Face cut the defender on catch if they close out hard on your low shoulder.
    Side Step 3
    If run off the line, take a side-dribble to open up a clean window for 3 instead of settling for a long 2.
    Wheel in Perimeter (Drift & Drag)
    Drift: Slide away from ball when ball drives toward you.
    Drag: Trail behind ball when driving away from you.
    Wheel in Dunker (Circle / I-Cut)
    Circle Cut: Weakside 5 cuts around rim for drive space.
    I-Cut: Strongside 5 pops straight up to clear baseline drive path.

    Pick & Roll Reads

    Tactical Reads
    "Make Defenders Trail" | "Force The Over" | "Paint Touch"

    Handler Reads

    • ★
      Score ➔ No one engages handler
    • ★
      Search ➔ E2 engages: look for Roll or Pop
    • ★
      Skip ➔ Collapsing tags: pass to open shooter
    • ★
      4 "S"s of PnR ⇒ See, Short, Skip, Shake

    PNR Techniques & Attack Reads

    Screening Basics
    Sprint to Screen: "Arrive Alone" to get separation.
    Back Pocket: Screen lower body to "Force the Over".
    Touch & Go: Slip screen immediately as defender hips turn.
    Wait for 2: Handler must wait for screener to get on 2 feet to avoid moving screen.
    Reject First: Handler rejects screen first to create space from defender.
    Shoulder to Shoulder: Handler comes off the screen shoulder to shoulder w/ screener to "Force the Over".
    Drop/Over Attack
    Hostage: Seal defender on back.
    Hang: Float dribble to freeze drop big.
    Gortat: Handler hostage O1 on back, screener seal X5 at rim.
    Engage Big: Pull X5 to open roll pocket.
    Drop/Under Attack
    Twist: Re-screen if defense goes under.
    Race the Under: Beat under defender to paint.
    Ice Attack
    Slip Roll: Dive screener to rim on Ice reject.
    Twist Screen: Flip into step up screen.
    Hand Game: Pop big, handback going over top.
    Switch Attack
    Race Switch: Drive soft switch downhill.
    Hard Hedge (Drive Wake): Attack switch foot angle.
    Yo-yo: Pass on switch, back up, catch and drive wake.
    Matchup Calls
    Mouse in House: Small guarding big in post.
    Moose on Loose: Big guarding guard on perimeter.
    Blitz Attack
    4 S Passes: Short roll, Shake (drag), See (reversal), Skip.
    Advance It: Flow pass and quick drive.
    Veer Attack
    Nash It: Keep dribble along baseline to pull out big.
    Warrior: Nash out, pass to wing, boomerang back.

      1-on-1 Defense

      On-Ball Principles
      "Pressure, Guard the Shell, Stay in Front w/o Fouling"

      Beliefs / Playbook

        On-Ball Techniques

        Guard Shell
        Strong Hand Attack: CHEST.
        Weak Hand Attack: RUN.
        Force "Flat Drives" outside the blocks and elbows.
        Stick Hand
        Early stick hand to his strong hand on every closeout.
        Chest
        Chest up: absorbing physical contact w/ your chest once square. SHOW HANDS to avoid fouls.
        Trace
        Keeping an active hand above the ball at all times to create pressure and reduce vision.
        Verticality
        When helping from RIM on scramble, must stay vertical and show hands. Jump inside Restricted Area w/ flexed core, stand ground outside it.

        Shell Team Terms

        Ball
        Must point and declare ball on every catch.
        Strong
        Defender guarding player next to ball (not "NAIL" though). Strong side corner. Responsible for shrinking and stunting.
        Rim
        Defender responsible for RIM HELP on attacks. Needs to learn how to stunt for help to make E2 decision harder.
        Low
        Bottom weak side defender. Responsible for Top Foot help on Rim Help. Has 2nd Pass on "Stay" rotation. Low Hole defender responsible for "BOMB/TRIP" help.
        Nail
        Top weak side defender. Sinks on baseline or slot drive, has 1st pass out. Stand outside paint near elbow to influence corner pass and take away slot cuts.
        Shrink
        Show bodies in driving lanes and in the paint. Force offensive players to make plays through multiple defenders (using BEN: Blocks, Elbows, Nail).
        Be In to Get Out
        Your BODY is "IN" position to help the on ball defender, but your MIND is intent on getting OUT to 3-point threats.
        Gap
        Supporting the on ball defender by being in the drive line.
        Deny
        Supporting the on ball defender while preventing the pass to your offensive player. Hand in the passing lane. DO NOT LET YOUR MAN GET THE BALL!
        Stunt
        Hand and foot fake by a help defender to fake help but get back to a shooter. Stunt Up The Line.
        Sink
        Weakside NAIL sinks to centerfield on a North-South drive.
        Stay Rotation
        Any time Rim Help is activated due to a paint drive:
        LOW: Top foot and takes the 2nd pass.
        NAIL: Sinks and takes the 1st pass.
        Peel
        When a man is beat and his hips are open below recovery line, the next help defender calls "PEEL" signaling a jump or switch.
        Hit-Find-Get
        Defensive rebounding technique. Shot goes up:
        HIT: Man w/ forearm.
        FIND: Turn, seal & find ball.
        GET: Pursue ball.
        Sandwich
        Send extra guard to double team weak side offensive rebounder. Guards check man. If he gets back, sprint to weakside.
        Pivot & Sprint
        When the ball moves, you sprint to help position on the ball's airtime. Be in proper position on the catch.

        Pick and Roll Defense

        PNR Coverages
        "The 5 Ts: Talk, Touch, Trace, Take, Tag"

        The 5 Ts Framework

        • ★
          TALK: Big calls early, loud, continuous (3x). "WEAK" or "ICE".
        • ★
          TOUCH: Guard impacts ball & forces into coverage; Big up to touch.
        • ★
          TRACE: Big takes away low pocket pass; Guard takes high pocket.
        • ★
          TAKE: Guard calls "BACK" and Big recovers to his man.
        • ★
          TAG: Weakside NAIL in but fans out. Weakside BASE is ultimate tag.

        PNR Coverages & Tactics

        Drop/Brown
        Big: Calls "LEFT/RIGHT" to direct on-ball defender. Plays centerfield (touch screener's inside shoulder), stabs at ball while remaining square between ball & rim (Ball-You-Basket). On flat middle screens, force to weak hand. No roller behind (Men-You-Basket).
        Dhandler: Forces handler to accept screen (shadow/blowup, under) until recovery to SQUARE.
        Flat/Grey
        Used against elite 3-point threats.
        Big: At the level (touches screener's back/outside shoulder), soft show, stab at ball and stay for a second.
        Low Hole: Activated for rolls.
        Dhandler: Forces handler to accept screen (shadow/blowup, under) until recovery to SQUARE.
        Blitz/Pink (Hard Hedge)
        Big: Blitz (slides w/ chest facing sideline and jab at ball), stay until dribble is picked up or retreat dribble, then sprint back w/ high hands to recover on roller.
        Dhandler: Forces into screen. Goes over screen under hedger.
        Ice/Blue
        On outer 1/3 of floor.
        Guard: Prevents ball from going middle (chest on middle side hip).
        Big: Drops (level depends on KYP) keeping Ball-You-Basket relationship. Switch to Drop if ball enters middle.
        Under/Green
        Dscreener: In drop to leave room for Dhandler to get under the screen.
        Dhandler: Goes under the screen and sprints ahead to get back to SQUARE.
        Switch/Gold
        Talk: Dscreener calls "TBD" to switch. Force ball into screen to eliminate rejection.
        Touch: Chest on shoulder.
        Take: Switch up/under.
        Peel
        Late switch in P/R below the PEEL line when guard cannot get back in front. Also used for emergency paint switches off pins & staggers.
        Peel vs Pick n Pop
        PEEL after the ball crosses the 3-point line threshold.
        Guard: Takes the popping big.
        Big: Defends the ballhandler.
        Double/Red (Hard Hedge)
        Dscreener: Doubles (slides w/ chest facing sideline).
        Dhandler: Forces into screen. Goes over screen to double.
        Stay
        Used for like-size screening. "STAY" tells Dhandler to square feet. "SWITCH" on screen contact. Chest on shoulder to eliminate slips.
        Squeeze
        Used vs pick-n-pop Big or non-shooting guard. Preserves matchups.
        Dscreener: Stays, bodies screener as high as possible.
        Dhandler: Goes under.
        Shadow
        Defender follows outside hip of offensive player through screens until they can get back to SQUARE or PEEL.
        Gap
        Dscreener: Gaps.
        Dreceiver: Whips under late and gets back to SQUARE w/ a high hand.
        Chase
        When Dscreener is in KYP Drop.
        Dreceiver: BUSTS OVER the handoff and stays attached. Force everything UPHILL & 1-WAY.
