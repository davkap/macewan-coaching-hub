# Scout Report: Alberta Golden Bears vs MacEwan Griffins
**Date:** TBD | **Tip:** TBD | **Venue:** TBD | **Style:** SYSTEMS

> Alberta swept MacEwan in our gym Nov 21-22 (W 80-69, W 87-71). They are deep, balanced, and aggressive on the offensive glass (17-19 OREB in those games). Isaac Simon (#3) leads the scoring; Nash Semeniuk (#9) is a high-volume bench creator; Caiden Kushnir (#13) is a +5 OREB kid off the bench. They take 30+ threes a game with wide variance, so the win condition is to stack possessions: block out and take away their second-chance points, then make them defend our PnR repeatedly with their non-PG defenders.

## 4. How we attack their D
1. PnR at Simon (#3). Make their top scorer guard 28+ minutes of screen action.
2. Hammer Yusuf (#5) and Waldron (#10) in space. PnR pop and ghost screens.
3. When Kushnir (#13) checks in for offense, run our 4 into a screen action. He has not shown lateral mobility on tape.
4. Take away the corner three. Our top-side help has to be honest; they will swing-swing-corner.
5. Play through the post early. Their bigs are not premium rim protectors.

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Defensive rebound rate above 75%. They had 17-19 OREB on us in the November sweep. That is the game.
2. Limit Isaac Simon to <18 pts. Force the ball out of his hands in late clock.
3. Win the variance battle from three. They take 30+ a game; sometimes they are 6-of-33. Defend, contest, and run them off the line.
4. Get Kushnir's bench minutes off-balance. When he subs in, we attack him in PnR immediately.
5. Cut their second-chance points to <12. Block out, gang rebound, finish defensive possessions.
