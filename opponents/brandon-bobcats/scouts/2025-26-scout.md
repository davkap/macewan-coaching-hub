1. Hammer the rim early. Their 6'9 (Duncan) is a freshman; force him into rotation and 2-foul trouble in Q1.

3. Make Hamberger and Gill guard sustained ball pressure off-ball; switch them on to our biggest wing in screening actions.
4. Attack closeouts. They surrendered 49% FG to Alberta — paint touches and kick-out 3s should be open.
5. Second-side actions. Get into a quick-hitter, then immediately re-screen on the weak side before the bigs settle.

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Win the paint. They are a 10-guard team with two 6'8 forwards and one freshman 6'9. Get 40+ paint points.
