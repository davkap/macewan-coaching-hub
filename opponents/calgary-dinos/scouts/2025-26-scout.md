## 4. How we attack their D
1. Pace. Push every miss and made FT. Big lineups are slowest in transition; get easy first-shot looks before they set.
2. PnR repeatedly at Francis (#23) and Sabaliauskas (#12). Make their 5s defend in space; they will switch and we can attack the seams.
3. Get bigs in foul trouble. Francis (240 lb) plays physical; bait him into block-charge and reach-ins on close-outs.
4. Spread shooters. They have length but if our 4 can shoot, we drag Sabaliauskas to the 3-point line.
5. Attack the freshman PG Dhaliwal in PnR. Make him guard our top guard in screen action repeatedly.
