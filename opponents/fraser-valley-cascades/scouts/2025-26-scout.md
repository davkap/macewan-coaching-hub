## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. **Win the OREB battle.** Plus-4.2 reb margin is their identity. Hit them first.
2. **Force live-ball TOs.** They are 2nd in CW in fewest TOs; if we can get them to 16+ we win possessions.

5. **Get to 80.** They give up 76+ regularly; if we hit 80 with average TO margin, this is a game.
