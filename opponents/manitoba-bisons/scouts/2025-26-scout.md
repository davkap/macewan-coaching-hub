1. **Quick early offence before their bigs set the half-court.** Their half-court D is locked in; transition is our window.

4. **Shoot quickly off catches.** Their closeouts are long; do not put it on the floor against a set defender.

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. **Cut their three-point volume.** They averaged 14 makes vs us in the sweep. We need them under 9.
2. **Win TO margin.** They take care of the ball. We have to manufacture extra possessions.
3. **First eight minutes.** They built leads early in both January games. Hold serve to halftime.

5. **No transition gifts.** Their half-court is hard enough; do not give them easy ones.
