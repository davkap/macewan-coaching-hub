# Scout Report: Mount Royal Cougars vs MacEwan Griffins
**Date:** TBD | **Tip:** TBD | **Venue:** TBD | **Style:** SYSTEMS

> Mount Royal in Year 2 under HC Josh Mullen has flipped the script from a 4-16 conference team in 2024-25 to a 13-7 conference team in 2025-26. They run a balanced guard-heavy attack (10 of 13 listed players are guards) led by 1st-year Canada West All-Rookie Jacob McLellan, returning 4th-year wing Sam Barnie (6'6), and senior PG Kole Scott (Northern Oklahoma JC transfer). Faruq Balarabe (London, England, ex-Lake Superior State NCAA D-II) is a real frontcourt addition at the 4. They split with Thompson Rivers in early January, swept MacEwan twice in November (84-60, 69-48), and hammered Saskatchewan twice. Expect tight, possession-conscious basketball  -  they grade out near 75 ppg both ways. Beat them by playing through size at the rim (they have only one true 6'8 in Pigoue) and by limiting McLellan in transition.

## 4. How we attack their D
1. Attack the rim. They have only one true 6'8 (Pigoue) and one 6'6 (Owoeye/Barnie/Balarabe). Get to the line.
2. Make freshman McLellan defend our PG in screen actions for 28+ minutes. He's their best young guard but he's a 1st-yr defending CW.
3. Keep Sam Barnie off the offensive glass. He's their best returning big-wing rebounder.
4. Push pace. They're 75 ppg both ways - mid tempo. Get easy first-shot looks before their set defense.
5. Live with Kole Scott pull-ups. Make him beat us with the dribble; deny Owoeye and Pigoue catches inside 17 ft.
