## 4. How we attack their D
1. Run them. They are the slowest team in CW. Score in the first 8 seconds before Thimm sets the half-court shell.
2. Drag Thimm out. Spread PnR with our 5 popping; he is uncomfortable on the perimeter at 6'10.
3. Attack the 5'8 (Linklater) when he is in. Get him on a wing in space.
4. Force second creators. If Bonsu sits or doubles off, the offense has nowhere to go. Pre-rotate help to him at every catch.
5. Crash the offensive glass. Thimm is a rim protector but only ONE of him; second-shot points are there.

## 5. How we defend their O
- **Top scorer:** Emmanuel Bonsu (Yr 5, Stonehill transfer) — defender: best on-ball wing — game plan: top-lock and force him left; pre-switch out of side PnR; he is the only true creator.
- **Ball-screen plan:** Hedge HARD on Bonsu PnR; force him to give it up. Drop on Mahadhi/Linklater PnR (low-volume creators).
- **Off-ball screens to be aware of:** Pin-downs and DHOs for Bonsu and Miller; chase over the top, no help underneath.
- **Tagger / weak-side rules:** Tag Thimm on the roll EVERY time. He is their lob and short-roll passer.
