## 4. How we attack their D
1. Attack switches early, before they set the shell. The first action gets them in rotation; the second action gets them in trouble.
2. Hunt Stinson (5'10) on switches. Get him on a wing or a 4 in the post.
3. No mid-range. They wall up at the rim (3.5 BLK/g) but they are mid-pack on threes allowed (35.6%). Live in the corner-three / rim-or-three diet.
4. Slip the screens. They switch heavy; fake the screen and slip the roller before contact.
5. Ball movement. Five-out, no dribble drives into a wall. Move the ball, find the open shooter on the second/third reversal.

## 5. How we defend their O

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Win the turnover battle. They steal it 8.4/g. If we go sub-13 TOs, we steal possessions back from a balanced team.
2. Slip switches. Force their length to recover; we don't beat them with iso.
3. Three-point volume. Their interior wall is real (3.5 BLK/g); thirty 3PA is the target.
4. Finish-or-foul at the rim. No floaters; either dunk it or kick out.
5. Defensive rebounding. They have six 6'6+ forwards. Block out, two-foot-jump.
