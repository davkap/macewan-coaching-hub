## 4. How we attack their D
1. Hunt Platz in space. Run side ball-screen with shooter as screener forcing him to switch onto our PG, then attack the closeout.
2. Crash 2 to the offensive glass every possession. Mutabazi is their best defensive rebounder, get him on the ball.
3. Reverse the ball quickly. Their gap pressure breaks if we make the 2nd and 3rd pass on time. Punish their steal-hunting with skip passes to the weak corner.
4. Attack Sipma (5-11) and any of the freshmen guards in PnR. Switch hunting on Smiens / Van der Waarde / Stewart.

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Win the offensive glass. Two crashers minimum, every possession.
2. Hold Platz to under 12 points. Force him to be a passer when he pops.
