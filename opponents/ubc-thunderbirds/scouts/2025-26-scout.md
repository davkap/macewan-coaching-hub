## 4. How we attack their D
1. Force Guzina into actions. Run side PnR / DHO at him to put him in space. If he stays in drop, take open mid-range pull-ups; if he hedges, slip the screener.
2. Get him in foul trouble early. He played only 30 of 40 GP last year, lean hard on attacking him at the rim.
3. Punish freshmen guards if they're on the floor. Switch hunting on Abera/Dhadda/Gauthier/Navarro.
4. Quick ball reversals. Their help loads strong-side, skip to the weak corner.
5. Run after every miss. They are bigger but not faster, get easy ones before help sets.

## 5. How we defend their O
- **Top scorer:** Nikola Guzina (#6) - defender: our best post defender + double on second-side dribble - game plan: Front the post on the strong side, weak-side X dig early. Make him a passer not a scorer.
- **Ball-screen plan:** Switch 1-4 actions. ICE side ball-screens involving Tomie or any wing handler. Make pull-up jumpers the answer, not paint touches.
- **Off-ball screens to be aware of:** Pin-downs for Goerzen (#10) and stagger screens for Tomie (#4). Top-lock and chase. [FILL FROM FILM]
- **Tagger / weak-side rules:** Low man tags the Guzina roll early. Recover to the corner shooter on the kick.

## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Limit Guzina to under 12 points and/or get him 3 fouls by halftime.
2. Win the turnover battle. They were even at 18.1/18.2 in 24-25, that is exploitable.
