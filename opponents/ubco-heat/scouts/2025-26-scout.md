## 4. How we attack their D
1. Press them. -3.3 TO margin in 24-25 + 11 first-years = forced TOs. Run our 1-2-1-1 every 3rd possession.
2. Crash the offensive glass. Two crashers minimum when freshman bigs (Reid/Malfair/Schertzer) are on the floor.
3. Hunt switches onto Sunday in space. He is their only proven rim protector, get him in PnR coverage and out of position.
4. Attack Tsaiger (5-10) and freshman backups in transition and PnR. Switch hunting whenever a freshman is on the floor.
5. Don't settle. Get to the rim, get to the line.

## 5. How we defend their O
- **Top scorer:** Jalen Shirley (#10) - defender: our best on-ball defender - game plan: Force right (off-hand). 94-ft pressure when fresh defender available. Hard hedge on his side ball-screens, make someone else beat us.
- **Ball-screen plan:** Hard hedge or trap on Shirley actions. Drop on Conde-handler PnR (he's a shooter not a downhill driver). Switch 1-4 elsewhere.
- **Off-ball screens to be aware of:** Pin-downs for Conde (#12). Top-lock at first cross. Don't let him catch with rhythm above the break.
- **Tagger / weak-side rules:** Standard tag/recover. Low man helps on Sunday rolls.
