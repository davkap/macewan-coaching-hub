# Scout Report: UNBC Timberwolves vs MacEwan Griffins
**Date:** TBD | **Tip:** TBD | **Venue:** TBD | **Style:** SYSTEMS

> UNBC under 14th-year HC Todd Jordan has been the Pacific Division basement program in recent years (2-30 overall in 2024-25, 6-25 in 2025-26 per usportshoops). They are guard-dominant  -  11 of 16 listed are guards  -  but they have real frontcourt: 6'11 3rd-yr Will Keyes, 6'9 2nd-yr Miller Davies (Chilliwack), 6'8 3rd-yr Iceland import Haukur Davidsson (ex-NMMI), and leading scorer 6'7 4th-yr Evgeny Baukin (Moscow, ex-Duchess Park PG). Their offense runs through Baukin (30-pt high vs UBCO Oct 26), Justin Sunga (24-pt high vs TRU Feb 7), and Isaiah Bias (25-pt high vs Victoria). They beat Alberta 78-77 on Oct 31 and beat MacEwan 84-76 in the Nisga'a Clash season opener  -  they can win at home in Prince George. Beat them with size at the rim, deny Baukin catches, and win the turnover battle.

## 4. How we attack their D
1. Pressure Baukin (#13). Top-lock him on every catch. He is the only proven 20-pt scorer they have.
2. Attack 6'11 Will Keyes in PnR. He cannot move his feet on the perimeter. Force him to defend in space.
3. Push pace. They have not won a road game in two years. Make them defend in transition.
4. Make Sunga (#2) and Bias (#4) be the primary scorers, not Baukin. They have shown 25+ pt games but inconsistent.
5. Win the turnover battle. They are -14 ppg overall margin per usportshoops; they will give us extra possessions.
