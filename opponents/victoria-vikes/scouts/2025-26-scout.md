## 7. Keys to victory (top 5)
1. Win the offensive glass against their size. Our 5 must put a body on Stefani-Thompson or Pereira every shot.
2. Get to the FT line. Their core (Boag, Pereira, James, Smid) is older. Foul trouble is leverage.
3. No straight-line drives. Their length recovers. Make it a side-to-side game with extra passes.
4. Take care of the ball. Their length turns live-ball TOs into transition rim attacks.
5. Match their physicality early. Veteran teams set the tone in the first 4 minutes.
