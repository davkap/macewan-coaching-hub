# Foundation Sources — Basketball Systems Grounding

A curated bibliography the `/scout` agent uses to ground its understanding of basketball systems. Not a reading list for the user (though it works as one) — it's a citation map so the agent's analysis is rooted in real coaching literature, not vibes.

---

## Coaching books (X's & O's canon)
- **Pete Newell — *Playing Big*** (post play, footwork, big-man fundamentals)
- **Bob Knight — *Knight: My Story* + Indiana motion offense materials** (motion principles)
- **Pete Carril — *The Smart Take From the Strong*** (Princeton offense)
- **Tex Winter — *The Triple-Post Offense*** (Triangle)
- **Dean Smith — *Basketball: Multiple Offense and Defense*** (passing game, run-and-jump)
- **Jay Wright — *Attitude*** (Villanova four-out, ball-screen continuity)
- **Brad Stevens — Butler/Celtics actions** (zoom, gut, Spain PnR, modern NBA actions)
- **Don Meyer — *Basketball Coach's Bible*** (drills, motion, fundamentals)
- **Mike Krzyzewski — *Leading with the Heart*** (defensive principles)
- **Hubie Brown — clinic notes** (set play installation, situational basketball)
- **Dick Bennett / Tony Bennett — Pack Line defense materials**
- **Jim Boeheim — 2-3 Zone treatises**
- **Chris Mack / John Beilein — 1-3-1 trap variations**

## Analytics / strategy
- **Dean Oliver — *Basketball on Paper*** (Four Factors, possession-based analysis — the foundation of all modern hoops analytics)
- **Kirk Goldsberry — *Sprawlball*** (modern shot-selection economics)
- **Ben Taylor — *Thinking Basketball* (book + YouTube)** (era-adjusted analysis, role frameworks)
- **Seth Partnow — *The Midrange Theory*** (NBA scouting methodology, applies to college)
- **Daryl Morey / Sam Hinkie public talks** — efficiency-first roster construction

## Foundational concepts the agent treats as baseline knowledge
- **Five out / four out / three out** spacing models
- **Ball-screen reads**: roll, pop, slip, ghost, snake, reject, re-screen
- **Coverages**: drop, ICE/blue, hedge/show, switch, blitz, weak
- **Closeouts**: hard / short / chase
- **Off-ball screens**: down, flare, back, pin, cross, stagger, elevator
- **Cuts**: basket, UCLA, Iverson, flex, ghost, 45
- **Defensive systems**: pack-line, switch-everything, no-middle, scram, pre-rotation, X-out
- **Zone offense principles**: short corner, high post, ball reversal, skip
- **Situational**: SLOB, BLOB, ATO, late-clock, FT down 2/3, hack-a-strategy
- **Transition**: numbered break, secondary action, drag screen, get action, early offense
- **FIBA rules nuances** vs NBA/NCAA: zone is legal, no defensive 3 seconds, 14-second reset on offensive rebound
