# Chicago Action

**One-line:** Pin-down → dribble hand-off (DHO). Cousin of zoom but starts with a pin instead of a down-screen.

## How it works
1. Wing in the corner receives a **pin-down** from the big at the elbow
2. Wing pops to the wing slot
3. Big follows up the floor and gives a **DHO** at the top of the key
4. Wing attacks downhill

## Differences from zoom
- **Chicago** starts with a pin from the elbow → wing comes UP
- **Zoom** starts with a down-screen in the lane → wing comes UP differently

Both end in a DHO. Different angles for the defender.

## Defense counters
- Switch both actions
- Top-lock the corner wing
- Veer-switch on the DHO

### Verification notes (Claude)
- The pin-down → DHO mechanic is well-documented and matches the file. The naming distinction the file draws between Chicago (pin-down to start) and Zoom (down-screen to start) is **not universal** — many coaches and the Coach Hoops/CoachTube sources use the two terms interchangeably. Treat the distinction as one school of terminology rather than a settled definition.
- I did not find a definitive origin/practitioner story for "Chicago" as a name. If you want to attribute it (e.g., to a specific NBA staff), do not rely on this file alone.
