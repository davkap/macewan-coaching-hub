# Hammer Action

**One-line:** A baseline-driving guard kicks to a weak-side **corner** shooter who just used a back-screen ("hammer screen").

## How it works
1. Guard drives baseline (often from a ball-screen)
2. As guard attacks baseline, the weak-side big sets a **back-screen** on the weak-side corner shooter's defender
3. Weak-side shooter slides UP to the wing — open corner 3 (or wing 3)
4. Driver kicks across baseline to the open shooter

## Why it works
Help defenders collapse on baseline drives. The weak-side defender is the help — back-screening him strands him in the lane.

## Variations
- **Hammer to corner** (classic) — shooter stays in corner
- **Hammer to wing** (lift) — shooter pops up to the wing
- **Double hammer** — both weak-side players involved

## Defense counters
- **Don't help on the baseline drive** if you scout hammer (give up the contested layup, deny the kick)
- **Switch the back-screen** so the guard's man takes the shooter
- **Tag down on the screener** to prevent the back-screen from being clean

## When to call it
- ATO vs help-heavy teams
- Out of SLOBs with a baseline angle

## Famous practitioner
- Gregg Popovich (Spurs) — hammered teams to death with this for two decades
