# Closeouts (Concept Reference)

How a defender approaches a shooter who has just received the ball.

## Types

### Hard closeout
- Sprint at the shooter, high hands, contest the shot
- Use vs **shooters** (high 3PT%) and vs **catch-and-shoot** situations
- Risk: shooter pump-fakes and drives by

### Short closeout
- Stop a step short of the shooter, hands high
- Concedes the shot, takes away the drive
- Use vs **non-shooters** (drivers) and vs **pack-line** schemes

### Chase closeout
- Sprint behind the shooter to deny the shot, force the drive
- Use when help is set behind you (you can force the ball into help)
- Risk: shooter steps through for a 3

## Universal closeout rules
1. **High hands** — never closeout with hands at sides
2. **Choppy steps** the last 2 strides — gather to react
3. **Inside foot up** — force them to your help direction
4. **Closeout to a spot, not a player** — the spot is where you want them to drive

## Schemes drive closeout choice
- **Pack-line:** short closeouts to non-shooters, hard to shooters
- **Switch-everything:** chase closeouts to deny mid-range
- **No-middle:** closeouts force baseline by inside-foot positioning
- **Drop coverage:** closeouts to the kick-out shooter need to be balanced — don't get blown by

## Common closeout breakdowns
- Defender flies past the shooter (over-aggressive hard closeout)
- Defender doesn't contest at all (lazy short closeout)
- Defender closes out without help (gets blown by, no rotation)

## Drill it
The 4-on-4 shell drill is the closeout drill — every rotation ends in a closeout.
