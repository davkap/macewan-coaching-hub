# Pack-Line Defense (Dick Bennett → Tony Bennett)

**Family:** half-court man-to-man variant. Conservative, gap-oriented, deny-the-paint philosophy.

## Core rule
**All four off-ball defenders stand inside an imaginary "pack-line" — about 16 feet from the basket (2 feet inside the 3-point arc).** This crowds the paint, denies all driving lanes, and dares the offense to shoot contested 3s.

## Principles
1. **No gaps to drive through.** Off-ball defenders are always in help position before the catch.
2. **No middle penetration. Ever.** Force everything baseline or sideline.
3. **No deny on the perimeter** (except in last 8 seconds of the clock). Allow the catch to keep your help structure intact.
4. **Hard close-outs short.** Close out short with high hands — give up the contested 3 to take away the drive.
5. **No help on post catches** at first. Make the post-up player beat us 1-on-1; everyone else stays attached.
6. **Charge-take mentality.** Pack-line teams take charges; help defenders are early.

## Ball-screen coverage
- **Drop coverage** is the default — big sags, guard fights over.
- **Hedge** late in clock or vs elite shooters.

## Strengths
- Smothers drive-and-kick teams
- Dominates rebounding (everyone is already inside)
- Great vs teams that can't shoot

## Weaknesses (how to attack pack-line)
- **Shooting** — pack-line gives up perimeter looks. Teams that shoot 38%+ from 3 punish it.
- **Skip passes** — the pack is locked inside; one swing-skip catches them shifting late.
- **High-post flash** — flash a big to the elbow and play inside-out (Princeton + pack-line is a clean attack).
- **Back-cuts** — pack-line denies aren't aggressive, but pack defenders are sagging — back-cuts on hard close-outs work.
- **Ball-screens with elite shooters** — drop coverage = pull-up 3 if your guard can shoot.
- **Off-ball screens to free shooters** — they don't switch aggressively.

## Scout signals (they're playing pack-line if…)
- 4 off-ball defenders are inside the arc on every catch
- Closeouts are short with high hands, not chase-out
- Big always drops on ball-screens
- They give up open 3s but no layups

## Famous practitioners
- Dick Bennett (Wisconsin–Green Bay, Wisconsin)
- Tony Bennett (Virginia — 2019 nat'l title)
- Sean Miller (Arizona)
- Many college programs across NCAA + U Sports
