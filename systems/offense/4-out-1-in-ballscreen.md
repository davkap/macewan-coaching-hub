# 4-Out 1-In Ball-Screen Continuity (Villanova / Jay Wright)

**Family:** spread ball-screen offense with one post player. The dominant U Sports / NCAA / NBA framework today.

## Spacing
4 perimeter players (PG, 2 wings, stretch 4), 1 post in the dunker spot or high post.

## Core principles
1. **Every possession starts with a ball-screen.** Drag, side, top, or step-up.
2. **Read the coverage, not the call.**
   - Drop → pull-up jumper or pocket pass to roller
   - Hedge → split, snake, or skip to weak-side corner
   - Switch → attack mismatch immediately
   - Blitz → short roll, 4-on-3 advantage
3. **Spaced corners are sacred.** Never let a defender help two passes away.
4. **Replace, don't crash.** When the roller goes, the weak-side wing lifts.

## Signature actions
- **Spain pick-and-roll:** ball-screen + back-screen on the screener's defender → roller is unguarded.
- **Zoom action:** down-screen into a dribble hand-off — punishes top-locking defenders.
- **Get action:** PG hands off to wing then sets the ball-screen for him.
- **Double drag:** two consecutive ball-screens at the top — attacks switch teams.
- **Ram screen:** big screens for the ball-screener's defender first → cleaner roll.
- **Ghost screen:** screener slips before contact — attacks aggressive hedges.

## Counters / answers
- **vs Drop coverage:** pull-up the mid-range, lob to roller in pocket, find weak-side shooter.
- **vs Switch:** attack mismatch — guard posts the switched big or big iso's the switched guard.
- **vs Blitz/Trap:** short roll the big to the FT line, 4-on-3 advantage, hit the open shooter.
- **vs Ice (forces side):** rescreen, or use a step-up screen on the other side.

## Scout signals
- Ball-screen on nearly every possession
- Stretch-4 lifting to the slot
- Constant corner spacing maintained
- Multiple actions in a single possession (chains)

## Famous practitioners
- Jay Wright (Villanova — 2 nat'l titles)
- Brad Stevens (Butler / Celtics)
- Mike Budenholzer (Bucks)
- Most U Sports programs run a version of this

### Verification notes (Claude)

- Brad Stevens and Mike Budenholzer are accurately characterized as ball-screen-heavy modern coaches; specific schematic attributions weren't deeply verified.
