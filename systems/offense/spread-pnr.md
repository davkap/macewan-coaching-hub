# Spread Pick-and-Roll (Modern NBA)

**Family:** the modern NBA's default offense — empty-side or middle ball-screen with maximum spacing.

## Spacing
**5-out** with the screener as the only player in motion. Four shooters spaced; one screener (often a stretch big) involved with the ball-handler.

## Core principles
1. **Empty the strong side.** Move the strong-side corner up so the ball-handler has a clean attack.
2. **Maximize the read.** With 4 shooters spread, every coverage gives up something.
3. **Shoot, drive, or kick.** Eliminate inefficient mid-range catches.

## Signature actions
- **Empty-side ball-screen** — strong-side corner cleared
- **Middle ball-screen with corners filled** — drag screen for the PG
- **Step-up ball-screen** — screen comes from a side angle

## Reads (covered in detail in `concepts/ball-screen-reads.md`)
- Pull-up 3 vs drop
- Pocket pass to the roller
- Skip to weak-side corner
- Reject if defense ICEs

## Counters / answers
- **vs Switch:** mismatch attack instantly
- **vs Blitz:** short roll → 4-on-3
- **vs Drop:** pull-up from elite shooter

## Scout signals
- Almost every possession is an isolation or a single ball-screen with 4 shooters spaced
- Stretch-5 lifting to the slot
- High 3PA + high rim attempts; few mid-range

## Famous practitioners
- Steve Nash–era Suns (origin)
- James Harden / Daryl Morey Rockets (extreme version)
- Modern NBA universally
